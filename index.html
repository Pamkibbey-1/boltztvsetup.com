BEFORE YOU BOOK

TV mounting FAQs

Answers about mounts, walls, cables and scheduling.

Get a free estimate
How much does TV mounting cost?

Estimated mounting prices start at $99 for TVs up to 55 inches, $129 for 33 to 60 inches, $179 for 61 to 75 inches and $229 for Over 75 inches. Use the planner to include your mount, wall and optional services. Confirm the final quote before booking. Tax, travel and nonstandard materials are separate.

Do I need to buy a mount first?

You can select “I already have a mount” or “Help me choose” in your plan. Compatibility depends on the TV’s weight, VESA pattern, wall construction and your preferred viewing angle. Confirm the mount and any supply costs before installation.

Can the wires be hidden?

Possible approaches include paintable surface covers or in-wall routing where suitable. The right option depends on your wall, outlets and cables. In-wall power requires an appropriate code-compliant solution; a TV power cord should not simply be fed through the wall.

What about a TV above the fireplace?

That needs a closer look at heat exposure, the TV manufacturer’s guidance, wall construction, cable routing and viewing height. Include the fireplace and wall details in your plan so suitability can be assessed before work is agreed.

Can I mount a TV in a rental?

Check your lease and get your landlord’s approval before making holes or changes to the wall. A surface cable cover may avoid opening the wall, but mounting still typically requires fixing points that may need repair when you move.

Which areas do you serve?

Serving Bentonville, Arkansas. Call Boltz TV Setup at (870) 293-5517 to confirm coverage for your address and appointment availability before booking.

Can I book an installation now?

Call Boltz TV Setup at (870) 293-5517 to request an installation in Bentonville, Arkansas. Your appointment and final price must be confirmed directly. The online planner creates a project summary; it does not submit a request or reserve a time.

What should I have ready for installation?

Know your TV model and size, check access to the installation area, and gather any mount and connected devices you already own. Clear the workspace and confirm placement and cable requirements with your installer before work begins.
